<<Stage 4>>

Goal: Create lcntMultiMedia-{assemble.cs,cbs.pcs,sbom.pcs} to allow for
sys/cbm/collective/induct/lcntMultiMedia/ --- everything LCNT needs to turn a presentation into a
narrated, captioned video: audio and video recording and editing, screen capture (OBS), forced alignment
and transcription, subtitles, and the =mmUnite= flow.

This package supplies the CBSs, sboms and assemble steps that a CBM leaf points at.
CBM sits on top of CBS: a leaf holds only a pointer (=capabilityName= + =cbsSpec=).

The capability of this stage is of capabilityType =induct=, in realm =collective= at:
sys/cbm/collective/induct/lcntMultiMedia

Relation to the other stages:
- It builds on [[Stage 2]] (lcntCore: LaTeX, HeVeA, fonts, evince). lcntMultiMedia checks that
  lcntCore is
  there and never reinstalls it. The leaf is materialized after lcntCore.
- It does not need [[Stage 3]] (lcntBases). =mmUnite= works inside one LCNT document directory.
- Packages are installed by the existing scripts or the existing sboms, as in Stage 2 (decision of
  2026-11-11); the scripts are the source of what is needed, a CBS verifies the result.
- One CBM, conditioned on the environment: Container, VM or Box (rawMetal, a pure box). Everything is
  core (nothing is "optional"); what is installed and checked depends on the environment, see [[4.11]].
  In a container the reveal presentation must still build, with no ability to record or play audio and no
  video recording.
